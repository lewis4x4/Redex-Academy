# The Aero X1100 Kit, Terminals & Fail-State

The Aero X1100 is a single-door controller-plus-reader. Know its terminals before you
wire, and confirm the door's fail-state before you energize.

<Callout tone="safety" label="Safety">
  The Aero relay is a switch, not a power source. Confirm the door's fail-state — and that an egress
  door is **fail-safe** — before energizing anything.
</Callout>

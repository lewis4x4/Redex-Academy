# Cable Families, the 100m Rule & PoE

Structured cabling has limits you cannot wish away. A Cat6 channel runs to a maximum
of **100 meters** end to end. Power over Ethernet delivers both data and power, but
the **PoE class** must match the device's draw.

<Callout tone="info" label="Note">
  The 100m channel includes patch cords on both ends — budget for them, don't pull the full 100m of
  horizontal run.
</Callout>

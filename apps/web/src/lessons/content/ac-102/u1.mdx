# Credentials, OSDP vs Wiegand & Lock Families

Readers talk to controllers over a wire protocol. **Wiegand** is the legacy one-way
signaling; **OSDP** is the modern, supervised, encryptable replacement. Match each
credential to the protocol its reader speaks, then spec each door's lock fail-state.

<Callout tone="safety" label="Safety">
  Lock fail-state is a life-safety decision. An egress door's lock must **fail-safe** (release on
  power loss). A non-egress security room may be **fail-locked** (fail-secure). Get this backwards
  and you either trap people or leave a door open.
</Callout>

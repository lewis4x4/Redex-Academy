# Login, 2FA & the Top-Bar Map

Your dealer login is your identity in the Partner Portal. Treat login hygiene
seriously — shared or sloppy credentials put every customer account at risk.

<Callout tone="info" label="Note">
  Logins are not user codes. A login authenticates a person to the portal; user codes control panel
  and door access for end users. Don't conflate them.
</Callout>

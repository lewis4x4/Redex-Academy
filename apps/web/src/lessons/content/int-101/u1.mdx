# Work OS at a Glance & the Job Journey

A job moves through a fixed lifecycle in Work OS: scheduled, dispatched, executed,
documented, closed. The **closeout quartet** — photos, test results, customer
sign-off, and as-built notes — is what turns "done" into "proven."

<Callout tone="info" label="Note">
  Closeout is not paperwork for its own sake. It is the evidence the field sign-off and the customer
  record are built on.
</Callout>

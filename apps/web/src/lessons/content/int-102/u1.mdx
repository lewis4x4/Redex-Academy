# Offline-First Field Execution

Sites have dead zones. The app keeps working offline: your work queues locally and
reconciles when you reconnect. Server-authoritative steps (a pass, a sign-off, a
badge) show a clear "will complete on reconnect" state — they are never faked offline.

<Callout tone="safety" label="Safety">
  Before you leave an offline job, confirm your queued work is captured. A tech cannot mint a pass
  on a plane — the server decides when you are back online.
</Callout>

# LP1501 vs LP1502, PoE & Reader Wiring

The Mercury LP1501 (single door) and LP1502 (two door) are powered over PoE — but
**PoE powers the controller, not the lock load**. The lock draws from its own supply,
and a long, thin home-run can drop the voltage below the lock's hold threshold.

<Callout tone="safety" label="Safety">
  Below its minimum hold voltage a mag lock chatters and can release unexpectedly — a life-safety
  failure. Always run the **will-it-hold** math before you commit a gauge and a run length.
</Callout>

# Ladders, LOTO, PPE & Electrical Safety

Before any device, you keep yourself and the customer safe. Set ladders at the
**4:1** ratio, lock out and tag out before touching a circuit, and wear the PPE the
task calls for.

<Callout tone="safety" label="Safety">
  Lockout/tagout is not optional and not negotiable — not for a manager, not for a deadline. If you
  cannot verify the circuit is de-energized, you do not work on it.
</Callout>

# Free Egress, §7.2.1.6 & the Headline Numbers

Egress is the rule that overrides every other access-control goal: people must be
able to **get out** without special knowledge, a key, or a credential.

<Callout tone="safety" label="Safety">
  Sensor-release (a request-to-exit) is **not** a substitute for the code-required release. A motion
  REX alone never satisfies free egress on a mag lock. The push-to-exit mounts at **40–48 inches**
  and releases for a minimum of **30 seconds**, and the lock must release on **fire-alarm**
  activation. The Authority Having Jurisdiction has the final word.
</Callout>

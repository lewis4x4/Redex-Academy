# Who Redex Is & the Five Work OS Pillars

Redex runs on **Work OS** — the single system of record for the job. Everything you do
in the field lands there: the customer, the site, the devices, the evidence, and the
sign-off. The five pillars are how a job moves from sold to closed-out and proven.

<Callout tone="info" label="Note">
  Courses here are not slides-and-a-quiz. You learn it, you rehearse it in a simulation, then you
  prove it in the field against a real job. See one, do one, prove one.
</Callout>
